# Capability Domain Catalog

This document lists the initial domains and groups defined for the System Builder Capability Catalog.

## Domains

Domains are high-level categories that group capabilities by business area.

- **Identity & Access**: Authentication, authorization, and profile management.
- **Organization & People**: Structures, roles, and human resource management.
- **Attendance & Requests**: Handling incoming requests, service desk, and intake.
- **Work & Execution**: Planning, executing, and tracking tasks and projects.
- **Assets & Maintenance**: Lifecycle management of physical and digital assets.
- **Inventory & Materials**: Stock control, movement, and warehouse management.
- **Procurement & Suppliers**: Sourcing, purchasing, and supplier relationship.
- **Sales & Relationship**: Customer management, CRM, and sales operations.
- **Financial**: Billing, payments, accounting, and financial control.
- **Documents & Records**: Content management, versioning, and archiving.
- **Schedule & Availability**: Time-based resource allocation and booking.
- **Quality & Compliance**: Inspections, audits, and standard enforcement.
- **Communication & Notifications**: Messaging, alerts, and notifications.
- **Data Analysis**: Analytics, reporting, and business intelligence.
- **Education & Training**: Learning management and certifications.
- **Health & Clinical**: Medical records, clinical workflows, and patient care.

## Groups

Groups categorize capabilities by their functional nature.

- **Core Management**: Fundamental entities and life-cycle management.
- **Operational Control**: Real-time execution and monitoring.
- **Resource Optimization**: Allocation and efficiency of resources.
- **Strategic Planning**: Long-term analysis and decision support.
- **Support Services**: Enabling functions that serve other capabilities.
