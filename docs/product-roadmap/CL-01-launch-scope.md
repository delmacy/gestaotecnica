# Commercial Launch Alpha Scope

## Launch Scope
- End-to-end event sourcing implementation.
- Basic Builder workflow publication.
- Workspace creation and isolated tenant administration.

## Personas
- **System Administrator**:
  - **Role**: Platform operations and monitoring.
  - **Primary Need**: Reliable deployment and rollback mechanisms.
  - **Success Metric**: Zero-downtime deployments.
- **Workflow Architect**:
  - **Role**: Business process design.
  - **Primary Need**: Intuitive visual builder for workflows.
  - **Success Metric**: Time to publish first workflow.

## Non-goals
- Advanced Analytics and Reporting dashboards.
- Custom third-party app integrations.
- Multi-region high availability setup.

## Validation Note
- Launch Scope maps to the objective of enabling initial commercial adoption.
- Personas map to the objective of ensuring the platform is usable by key stakeholders.
- Non-goals map to the objective of maintaining focus and delivering MVP features efficiently.
